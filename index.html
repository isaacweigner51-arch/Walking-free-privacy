Walking Free Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 12, 2026
Last Updated: August 12, 2026

Walking Free respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Walking Free mobile application.

Information We Collect

Walking Free is designed to help users track their sobriety and recovery progress. Depending on the features you use, the app may process information you provide, including:

  • Sobriety or recovery start date
  • Recovery milestones
  • App preferences
  • Account information, if account functionality is provided
  • Other information you voluntarily enter into the app

We do not sell your personal information.

How We Use Information

Information processed by Walking Free may be used to calculate sobriety time, track recovery milestones, save preferences and progress, provide app functionality, and improve the reliability of the app.

Sensitive Information

Sobriety dates and recovery-related information may be considered sensitive information. Walking Free uses this information only as necessary to provide the recovery and sobriety-tracking features requested by the user. We do not sell recovery information or use it for targeted advertising.

Data Storage

Information may be stored locally on your device or through services used by Walking Free to provide app functionality. Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed through the app.

Third-Party Services

Walking Free may use third-party technology or service providers necessary to operate, distribute, maintain, or improve the app. These providers may process limited information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Walking Free is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data and Account Deletion

Users may delete information stored locally by removing the app or using available deletion functionality within the app.
